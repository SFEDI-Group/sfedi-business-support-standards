# Changelog

What changed in each version of the SFEDI Business Support Standards released in this repo, newest first. Earlier versions were published on the SFEDI website from October 2026.

## Version 1.9 (November 2026)

Released 17 November 2026. The first release as open data.

- 44 Standards: 8 Core, 27 Specialist, 4 Business Information and 5 Introduction to Business Support, with 455 performance requirements, 418 knowledge requirements and 11 professional behaviours
- Since version 1.8, the introductory sections say SFEDI Specialist Programmes in place of Recognised Programmes. No code or requirement changed

## Withdrawn codes

These codes were withdrawn before the first release in this repo. They will never be used again.

| Codes | Withdrawn |
|---|---|
| C5.K9 | October 2026, before it was published |
| C4.8 to C4.13 and C4.K6 to C4.K9 | Version 1.6, November 2026 |
| C6.K8 to C6.K17 | Version 1.6, November 2026 |

Version 1.6 renumbered the remaining C4 and C6 requirements, once. Codes have been permanent since then.
